Rules and facts

1. How a stage works

  • There are five stages, one for each of Lessons 1 to 5. Play them all with Start the climb, or tap one stage to work on it alone.
  • Each stage opens with a short lesson in three to five parts.
  • One progress-check question follows. It is not timed and scores no points; get it wrong and you can reread the lesson and try again.
  • Then comes a 10-question quiz. After every answer you see the worked solution and the idea behind it.
  • Finished stages stay open. Tap one to reread the lesson or take a fresh quiz.

2. The mountain

  • Five levels: 1 Warm-up and 2 Core are underwater, 3 Standard is sea level, 4 Challenging and 5 Expert climb the mountain.
  • Every quiz starts at sea level.
  • Two correct answers in a row lift you one level. One wrong answer drops you one level.
  • You cannot go above Level 5 or below Level 1.
  • Expert questions are multi-step problems. Expect to think, not just substitute.

3. The clock

  • Every quiz question has a typical time: how long a prepared student usually needs.
  • Relaxed: the clock keeps running and shows how far over you are. You can still score.
  • Exam pace: when the typical time runs out, the answer is shown and the question scores zero.
  • Pause clock stops the timer. Opening Stickers, Scores, Progress or Rules mid-quiz pauses it too.

4. Points and stars

  • A correct answer earns 20 points for each level of the question (Level 3 = 60 points).
  • Using the hint halves those points.
  • Pace bonus: +10 if you answer within 60% of the typical time, +5 within the typical time.
  • Streak bonus: +5 for the second correct answer in a row, +10 for the third, up to +20.
  • A wrong answer scores nothing and resets the streak.
  • Every 100 points you earn gives one star. Stars never expire.

5. Answering

  • Numerical answers are accepted within 2% of the correct value (a little more when you read a value off a graph).
  • Type powers of ten as 3.0e8 or 3.0x10^8.
  • Give your answer in the unit shown next to the box. Convert kHz, MHz, cm and ms first if you need to.

6. Stickers and scores

  • Spend stars in Stickers. Tap a sticker you own to wear it as your badge.
  • Scores lists everyone who has played on this device, with their badge. Scores is shared: everyone who plays online appears on it. Only the teacher can reset it, with a secret code.
  • Progress shows your best result and highest level for each stage.

7. Saving

  • Everything is saved in this browser, under the name you type. Use the same name to carry on.
  • Leaving mid-quiz keeps your place. Tap Home for the main page; tap Play or Resume to return to the quiz.
  • End needs two taps and drops that quiz's answers so far.
  • Clearing the browser's data, or opening the game on another device, starts afresh.
